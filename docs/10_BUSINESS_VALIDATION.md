# 10 - Business Validation Plan

Software can be built in hours; commercial evidence cannot.

## 0-30 days
Measure:
- leads
- fit calls
- paid audits
- audit revenue
- audit conversion
- first pilot/deployment wins
- source/vertical response

Decision question: will businesses pay RH for the audit and implementation path?

## 31-60 days
Measure:
- audit -> deployment conversion
- average deployment value
- delivery hours
- direct cost
- gross margin
- baseline vs realized client outcomes
- first retainer adoption

Decision question: can RH deliver profitably and prove value?

## 61-90 days
Measure:
- repeatability by vertical
- sales cycle
- reusable implementation percentage
- rep performance
- partner/referral performance
- CAC if paid acquisition starts
- MRR and expansion

Decision question: which vertical/package should receive sales hires, ads, and deeper productization?

## Core rule
Do not delay shipping software to wait for 30/60/90-day milestones. Those milestones are for market learning and scaling decisions.
