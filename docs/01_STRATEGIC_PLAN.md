# 1. Executive decision

RH Agentic Operations becomes an immediate commercial priority inside RH
Digital Agency. It is not positioned as generic “AI automation” or as
installation of a specific model. RH sells operational redesign plus
implementation of agentic capacity: mapping work, simplifying processes,
deciding what stays human, and deploying agents with controls and
measurable outcomes.

# 3. RH Agentic Operations Framework

# 5. Audit as the commercial heart

The audit is not a generic AI assessment. It converts operating reality
into a prioritized investment plan. It should identify people,
processes, tools, time consumed, bottlenecks, error points, delays,
revenue leakage, data availability, decision boundaries and candidate
responsibilities for agents.

## Required audit outputs

- Executive summary and business objective.

- Current-state process inventory and maps.

- Classification: Human only / Automate / Agent-ready / Redesign first.

- Agentic Opportunity Score for each candidate process.

- Priority matrix based on impact, difficulty, risk and time-to-value.

- Baseline economics: hours, labor cost, response time, backlog, errors
  and revenue leakage where measurable.

- Recommended pilot with scope, controls, tools, expected KPIs and
  estimated ROI.

- 90-day implementation roadmap.

# 6. Opportunity scoring model

| **Dimension**             | **What RH evaluates**                                     | **Direction**                     |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Economic impact           | Cost saved, capacity gained, revenue protected or created | Higher is better                  |
| Frequency / volume        | How often the process occurs                              | Higher is better                  |
| Repetitiveness            | Consistency of inputs, steps and outputs                  | Higher is better                  |
| Data availability         | Whether required information is digitally accessible      | Higher is better                  |
| Rule clarity              | Whether acceptable actions can be defined                 | Higher is better                  |
| Human judgment            | Need for subjective/professional decisions                | Lower is better for autonomy      |
| Error cost                | Consequence of incorrect action                           | Lower favors autonomous execution |
| Implementation difficulty | Integrations, systems and exceptions                      | Lower is better                   |
| Time-to-value             | Time until measurable benefit                             | Shorter is better                 |

The score ranks opportunities; it does not override risk. High-impact
processes with high professional judgment or error cost remain
human-controlled or approval-gated.

## US commercial role

The first US sales hire should be a Business Development / Solutions
Representative, not an AI engineer. English fluency is mandatory;
bilingual is preferred. The role prospects, networks, runs structured
discovery, sells the paid audit, presents proposals and closes with
support from delivery. Compensation should initially protect fixed burn
and reward closed revenue, with worker classification and commission
structure reviewed correctly.

# 10. Sales process and qualification

Standard sales motion: Lead → 15-minute Fit Call → Paid Audit → Audit
Presentation → Pilot or Deployment → 30-day Measurement → Optimization
Retainer.

## Client qualification

- Sufficient transaction/process volume to generate ROI.

- Repeatable work with identifiable owners and inputs.

- Meaningful business pain: time, cost, backlog, lost leads, slow
  response or errors.

- Minimum digital maturity or willingness to digitize.

- Accessible data and systems.

- Management willingness to change processes and define approvals.

- Economic capacity to purchase an audit and implementation.

# 11. Delivery and control principles

- Do not promise “headcount reduction” as the primary value proposition;
  sell capacity, responsiveness and execution leverage.

- Client accounts, data and subscriptions remain under client ownership
  wherever practical.

- Every agent has a defined responsibility, allowed actions, prohibited
  actions, approval thresholds and escalation path.

- Every deployment has logs, monitoring, rollback and a named human
  owner.

- Regulated/professional judgment stays with authorized humans.

- Baseline metrics are captured before deployment so ROI can be
  demonstrated afterward.

- Technology choice is implementation-specific; RH sells the operating
  outcome, not a vendor.

# 18. Strategic conclusion

RH Agentic Operations should be treated as a new high-ticket product
line with the potential to become a repeatable operating system for
multiple verticals. The immediate goal is not broad geographic expansion
or a large technology build. The goal is to prove a paid audit, convert
it into measurable deployments, capture the resulting IP, and then scale
distribution. Growth Products remain valuable as a self-service and
acquisition layer; traditional RH services remain available where they
fit. All three reinforce one positioning: RH Digital Agency as a growth
partner that combines business process expertise with modern AI
execution.
