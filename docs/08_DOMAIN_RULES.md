# 08 - Domain Rules

## RH Agentic Operations Framework
DISCOVER -> MAP -> SIMPLIFY -> DELEGATE -> DEPLOY -> CONTROL -> MEASURE -> SCALE

## Commercial ladder
1. Paid Agentic Operations Audit
2. Pilot
3. Full Deployment
4. Ongoing Optimization / Retainer
5. SMB Starter for standardized lower-complexity use cases
6. Growth Products as lower-ticket acquisition/monetization layer

## Audit principle
Fit call may be free. Consulting is not free by default. The audit is paid and may be credited against implementation if contracted within the commercial window defined by RH.

## Agent design principle
Sell operational capacity and outcomes, not number of bots/agents.

## Human-boundary principle
Professional judgment stays human; operational execution can become agentic.

### Legal / Paralegal boundary
Allowed examples:
- administrative intake
- document collection
- appointment scheduling
- reminders
- status updates based on authoritative recorded information
- billing/collections administration
- internal tracking and escalation

Prohibited in autonomous agent scope:
- legal advice
- legal strategy
- interpretation of law for the client
- substantive legal recommendations
- autonomous regulated filings/decisions

### Dental boundary
Allowed examples:
- scheduling and rescheduling
- recalls and no-show recovery
- confirmations
- forms/admin collection
- status updates based on authoritative information
- reviews and administrative follow-up

Prohibited in autonomous agent scope:
- diagnosis
- clinical advice
- symptom interpretation
- treatment recommendations
- clinical judgment

## Initial vertical priority
1. Contractors / Home Services
2. Beauty / Salons (medium-size)
3. Legal Admin - premium, admin-only
4. Dental Admin - premium, admin-only

Real estate is intentionally not an initial RH vertical. Distribution may later be used to commercialize or partner around JP's existing real-estate developments/products rather than duplicate them.

## Productization goal
Move toward 80% reusable / 20% custom delivery by vertical.

Each deployment should produce reusable IP when appropriate:
- discovery questions
- process maps
- role cards
- instructions/prompts
- integration recipes
- controls
- QA tests
- onboarding checklists
- KPI definitions
- sales proof/case-study material
